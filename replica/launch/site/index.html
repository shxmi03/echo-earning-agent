How it works

1

Put your phone on the music stand.

Any acoustic or digital piano. Handspan tunes itself to your piano in about 30 seconds. With a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard, it reads the keys directly.

2

Play the lesson.

Each note turns green when it's right and on time. The hint says which note, which hand and which finger.

3

Fix the bar, not the song.

Miss something and Handspan loops just that bar at a tempo you choose, then puts it back into the piece.

Built for the part where you actually learn.

Recognition you can trust

Tuned to your piano, octave-correct, and a wrong note never counts, even inside a chord. If it gets one wrong, tap "I played that right" and it learns.

Loop the bar you keep missing

No restart from the top. Handspan loops the bar that went wrong, at the tempo it went wrong, until it's yours.

Timing, not only notes

A separate timing score shows each note as early, on time or late. Rhythm is half of reading music.

The why, in 30 seconds

A short card before each new idea: rests, time signatures, key signatures. Real terms, explained once.

Your sound stays on your phone

Note detection runs on the device. No recording of your playing leaves it.

Questions

Do I need a real piano?

Yes, for now. Any acoustic piano or digital keyboard with at least 61 keys. A MIDI cable or Bluetooth MIDI is the most accurate, but the microphone works on its own.

I already read a little. Do I have to start from middle C?

No. Take the 2-minute placement, or test out of any course by playing its final piece.

Is it for kids?

It's made for adults first. Kids do well with it too, because a mistake never sends them back to the start and there are no zero-star screens. Household gives each child their own profile.

What happens to my playing?

It's analysed on your phone and never uploaded. We store your results, such as which lesson and how many notes were on time, so they sync across your devices.

What does the trial turn into?

The plan you picked, at the price shown next to the button, on the date shown next to the button. Monthly is selected unless you choose yearly. We send a reminder 2 days before.

How do I cancel?

Settings › Subscription in the app, one tap. Or in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
