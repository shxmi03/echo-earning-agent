Using Handspan

Handspan teaches piano using your own acoustic or digital piano. You need an iPhone or iPad and, for the app, an account signed in with your email. You're responsible for keeping access to that email.

Free and paid plans

  • The whole first course is free, with no time limit.
  • Solo and Household are subscriptions sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and the price is shown before you confirm.
  • Monthly and yearly plans start with a 7-day free trial. We remind you 2 days before the first charge.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. Cancel any time in Settings › Subscription in the app, or in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its rules.

Household

A Household plan covers up to 5 learner profiles on one account, for people who share it.
