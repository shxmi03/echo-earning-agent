Handspan

Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026. Handspan is not released yet. This page covers the website and waitlist today, and the app once it launches.

The short version

  • Your playing is analysed on your phone. Audio from the microphone never leaves the device and is never recorded.
  • We store only what the app needs to work: your email, your learner profiles and your lesson results.
  • No ads, no ad tracking, and we don't sell or share your data for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app's Settings, in one step.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address and the date you joined. We use it only to tell you when Handspan is available. Visitors can add an address but can't read the list. To be removed, write to the contact address below and we'll delete it.

What the app stores

WhatWhy
Email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code and keep your progress on all your devices.
Learner profiles: a name you choose, adult or child, how you play (microphone, MIDI or on-screen keys), your learning path and experience levelTo set up lessons for each learner. Household plans can have up to 5 profiles.
Lesson results: which lesson, when, accuracy and timing scores, stars, tempo and which hands you playedTo show progress, unlock the next lesson and keep your weekly streak.
Subscription status from Apple: plan, renewal date and whether a trial is activeTo know which lessons you can open. We never see your card or payment details.
Settings stored only on your phone: reminder time, input latency, display optionsTo remember your choices. They don't leave the device.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and the app has no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Microphone and Bluetooth

The app uses the microphone to hear which notes you play, and Bluetooth only to connect a MIDI keyboard if you choose to. The sound is turned into notes on the device in real time and then discarded.

How long we keep it

Account data stays until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your profiles, results and streaks at once. Waitlist addresses are deleted after we send the launch email, unless you sign up. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes.

Your rights

If you're in the EU, the UK or anywhere with similar laws, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can object to how we use it. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Croatia that is AZOP (azop.hr).

Children

Handspan is made for adults. Children can use it as a learner profile set up by a parent on the parent's account. We don't knowingly create accounts for children under 16.

Contact

A contact email will be added here before the app launches.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and, for account holders, tell you in the app before it takes effect.
